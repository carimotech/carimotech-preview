Cari-On Learning

Applied ML and control training on real hardware — for student batches and for faculty. Four formats, so you can pick the one that fits your calendar and your budget line, not the other way round.

½ Day

Primer

See the stations run, ask anything, decide whether they fit your syllabus. No commitment.

2 Days

Crash Course

Compressed and hands-on. Everyone leaves having built and tested one working model.

3 Days

Deep Dive

The whole pipeline, properly — data audit to deployed model, ending on an independent challenge.

5 Days

Lock In

Bring your own problem or dataset. A full week, no distractions, and you leave with a result.

Run at SINE, IIT Bombay — or at your campus

Separate cohorts for students and for faculty. Certificates state your own measured result, not attendance.
