AI Products

CAPMAC AI Suite

Our flagship suite of customizable, AI-driven technologies designed to address complex industrial challenges and provide intelligent data processing across multiple domains.

01

Video Analytics

Hundreds of cameras, nobody watching. Footage only gets looked at after something has already gone wrong. CAPMAC watches every stream at once and indexes what it sees — so "find the moment that gate was left open" takes seconds, not an afternoon.

Under the hood: CUDA-accelerated pattern recognition with video and audio indexing — hundreds of concurrent streams on a single machine.
02

Agentic RAG Systems

The answer is in a 400-page standard operating procedure, an old report, and three spreadsheets — and the person who needs it can't write a database query. CAPMAC lets them ask in plain language and get an answer with the source attached.

Under the hood: Retrieval Augmented Generation with locally-running LLMs. The model runs on your own machine — no data leaves your network.
03

Predictive Maintenance (PdM)

Equipment doesn't fail silently. It changes how it vibrates, how much current it draws and how warm it runs, weeks before it stops. CAPMAC reads those changes and tells you which machine needs attention — so you plan the shutdown, instead of the shutdown planning you.

Validated: Proven through rigorous testing with BPCL and industrial partners in the Oil & Gas and Chemicals sectors.
04

Autotuning PID Control (AT-PID)

Most plants are running loops that were tuned once, at commissioning, years ago — and have drifted ever since. Operators quietly put them in manual. CAPMAC retunes them while they run, so the loop holds setpoint without anyone babysitting it.

Deployed: Running on Yokogawa and Honeywell DCS at BPCL and Aditya Birla. Level, flow, temperature, pressure and speed loops; OPC-DA/UA integration, with edge deployment on STM32-class MCUs planned.
