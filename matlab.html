Your MATLAB® licence is already paid for.
We give it something to spin.

Most control and ML labs teach on simulations. Students tune a controller that never touches a motor, and the theory stays abstract. These two benchtop stations fix that — the same MATLAB® your department already runs, now driving real hardware. One MATLAB® installation runs both.

Ecosystem 01

CARIMO AI Ecosystem

Machine learning you can touch

The problem with teaching ML on a downloaded dataset: it's already clean. Students never meet noise, drift, or a sensor that disagrees with itself. Here they spin a motor, collect the data themselves, and build the model on it — the full pipeline, end to end, without writing a line of code.

What's in it ML Station · AIMX Advanced · CARI Notebooks · 68 guided experiments

CARIMO Control Ecosystem

Three controllers. One motor.

Textbooks tell students PID, Fuzzy and MPC are different. On this station they find out how different — same motor, same disturbance, three controllers, and the gap between them shows up on the plot. Every Simulink® model is open and editable, never a black box.
